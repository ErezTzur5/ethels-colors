Choose a picture, then select a numbered color from the palette. Its unfinished areas turn gray. Tap those matching areas to reveal the color. Hold a numbered color in the palette to smoothly move to an unfinished matching area, then tap to paint. There’s no timer and no penalty for tapping somewhere else.
