Your coloring stays on your device

Painting progress, favorites and app preferences are saved locally. We don’t require an account or upload your paintings or progress to a game server. Cloud sync is not currently provided.

Removing the app may remove this local data. You can manage sound, music, haptics, auto-next and appearance in the app’s Settings.

Advertising and consent

The first family beta has advertising disabled. A separate development configuration uses Google’s test advertisements, which don’t earn advertising revenue.

When advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform process information to provide advertisements, manage consent and prevent fraud. This can include device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, device identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics.

We request applicable consent before advertisements. Where Google requires a privacy-options control, it appears in Settings. Coloring remains available if an ad can’t load, and full-screen ads don’t interrupt an active painting.

We don’t implement cross-app tracking or request Apple’s tracking permission in the current configuration. Adding that behavior would require a separate implementation and disclosure review.

Read Google’s privacy policy

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. Please don’t send passwords, payment information or sensitive personal information.

For help or privacy questions, contact erezur@outlook.com.

This website

This website doesn’t use advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or a sign-up form. Its buttons and artwork previews work locally in your browser. Our fonts and images are served with the website rather than loaded from third-party font or image services.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for security and delivery. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that hosting activity.

GitHub’s privacy statement

Our audience

Ethel’s Colors is a general-audience relaxation and coloring app. It isn’t specifically marketed as a children’s service. The app has no chat, social profile or user-content upload.

Advertising settings and the store age rating must reflect the released build and applicable requirements.

Your choices and policy updates

You can review applicable advertising choices through Privacy choices in Settings when available. You can also contact us with questions about personal information supplied through support.

This notice describes the current beta implementation. Before public release, we’ll check the privacy notice and store disclosures against the final advertising configuration. Changes will be reflected on this page.
